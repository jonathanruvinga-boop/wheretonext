Dial-a-Delivery (DAD) Zimbabwe

Operated by Simbisa Brands, Dial-a-Delivery is Zimbabwe's household delivery network. Whether you're resting in a Harare guesthouse, relaxing in a Victoria Falls Airbnb, or staying in Bulawayo, you can get freshly prepared meals and groceries delivered right to your lodge gate or door.

Popular Restaurant Brands Available for Delivery:
Pizza Inn Chicken Inn RocoMamas Nando's Zimbabwe Steers Flame-Grilled Creamy Inn & Baker's Inn Galito's Peri-Peri
Order on Dial-a-Delivery Web Portal → Call Hotline: +263 8677 004 004
Supported Cities & Operational Zones
Harare, Bulawayo, Mutare, Gweru, Chitungwiza, and Victoria Falls. Typical delivery time: 35–55 minutes. Payment accepted in USD cash, EcoCash, and Visa/Mastercard.
How Travelers Use Dial-a-Delivery:
Step 1
Choose Brand & Meal

Visit dialadelivery.co.zw or dial +263 8677 004 004. Pick from Pizza Inn, RocoMamas, Nando's or local menus.

Step 2
Enter Accommodation Address

Provide your hotel name, Airbnb street address, or lodge room number with your local WhatsApp contact phone.

Step 3
Pay & Receive at Gate

Drivers arrive on branded motorcycles. Pay in crisp USD cash or via international card upon delivery.

Step 4
Alternative Grocery Couriers

For fresh organic farm groceries in Harare, use Fresh in a Box (freshinabox.co.zw) or local Yango Delivery.
